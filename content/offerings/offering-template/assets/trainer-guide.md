## Delivery guidance

### Opening

- State the purpose, learning outcomes, agenda, time boxes, and support route.
- Confirm that participants can access the environment before beginning the
  hands-on segment.
- Establish working agreements for questions, breaks, collaboration, and
  recording.

### Instruction and demonstration

- Connect each concept to the participant scenario before demonstrating it.
- Demonstrate only the essential path; pause at planned checkpoints.
- Explain expected results and common mistakes without exposing sensitive data.
- Use knowledge checks to adapt pacing before participants work independently.

### Hands-on facilitation

- Give the goal, constraints, time box, starting point, and completion evidence.
- Ask participants to work from the written instructions, then coach rather
  than take over their environment.
- Check progress at each checkpoint and pair participants who need support.
- Keep an issue log containing the symptom, workaround, and follow-up owner.

### Train-the-trainer adaptation

- Model the facilitation language and explain why each agenda segment exists.
- Include a teach-back or micro-delivery exercise with structured feedback.
- Provide a handoff checklist so new trainers can validate their own readiness.

### Hackathon adaptation

- Publish challenge criteria, constraints, submission format, and judging rubric.
- Schedule mentor office hours and visible progress checkpoints.
- Reserve time for team presentations, judging, recognition, and reflection.
